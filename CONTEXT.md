# Berlin singings

The shared record of which songs the Berlin singers lead, and when.

## Language

**Singing**:
One gathering of the singers on one day, under one event name (for example "Thursday Singing at Refugio").
_Avoid_: session, meeting

**Call**:
One song led by one or more **Leaders** at a **Singing**, in order of entry.
_Avoid_: entry, lesson, row

**Leader**:
A singer who stands in the middle and calls a song.
_Avoid_: caller, singer (when meaning the person who led)

**Minutes**:
The ordered list of **Calls** of a **Singing**. The Google Sheet holds the minutes of all singings, one sheet per book and year.

**Minutes taker**:
The singer who records the **Minutes** during a **Singing**.
_Avoid_: secretary, scribe

**Draft minutes**:
**Minutes** recorded in the app on one device, not yet sent to the sheet. The **Minutes taker** can fix and reorder **Calls** freely until they send.

**Handover**:
Passing **Draft minutes** to another **Minutes taker** as readable text, which their device imports to continue the same **Singing**.
_Avoid_: share, transfer, sync

**Send**:
The one step at the end of a **Singing** that appends the **Draft minutes** to the sheet. After a send, the minutes belong to the sheet and fixes are made there.
_Avoid_: sync, upload, submit

## Relationships

- A **Singing** has one set of **Minutes**
- **Minutes** are an ordered list of **Calls**
- A **Call** has one or more **Leaders** and one song
- A **Singing** usually uses both books: one book in the first half, the other after the break

## Flagged ambiguities

- The sheet is the system of record. Others read and edit it, so the app sends new **Calls** to the sheet and never replaces it.
